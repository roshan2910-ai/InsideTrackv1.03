Why journalism archives matter

INSIDE TRACK EDITORIAL DESK · SAMPLE ARTICLE

A public record is more than a collection of old headlines. It is a way to revisit what was known, what was missed and how public understanding changed over time. Preserving original reporting helps readers compare claims with evidence, follow corrections and understand the decisions that shaped a story. A responsible archive records provenance: publication date, author, outlet, original URL, supporting documents and any later updates. It should distinguish the original work from commentary added by the museum. The archive is not a verdict on history; it is an invitation to examine the record.
