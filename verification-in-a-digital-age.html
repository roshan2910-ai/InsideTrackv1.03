Verification in a digital age

INSIDE TRACK EDITORIAL DESK · SAMPLE ARTICLE

Digital information travels quickly, but speed does not make a claim reliable. Verification begins with provenance: who created the material, when it first appeared and whether the source can be authenticated. Reporters should corroborate imagery and video, inspect context, seek independent confirmation and clearly label uncertainty. A transparent correction policy is part of this work. When an archive republishes or references digital material, it should retain the original source, access date and any known changes. Trust is built by showing readers how a conclusion was reached.
